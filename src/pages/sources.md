The figures on these pages are drawn from the standard published references. Production totals and specifications **vary between sources**, often considerably. Where they conflict, we have tried to give the most commonly cited figure. Corrections, with a citation, are always welcome.

## Standard References

- Coates, Earl J., and Dean S. Thomas. *An Introduction to Civil War Small Arms.* Gettysburg: Thomas Publications, 1990.
- Bilby, Joseph G. *Civil War Firearms: Their Historical Background and Tactical Use.* Conshohocken, Pa.: Combined Books, 1996.
- Davis, Carl L. *Arming the Union: Small Arms in the Civil War.* Port Washington, N.Y.: Kennikat Press, 1973.
- Flayderman, Norm. *Flayderman's Guide to Antique American Firearms and Their Values.* Many editions.
- Hess, Earl J. *The Rifle Musket in Civil War Combat: Reality and Myth.* Lawrence: University Press of Kansas, 2008.
- Lewis, Berkeley R. *Notes on Ammunition of the American Civil War, 1861–1865.* Washington: American Ordnance Association, 1959.

## Period Manuals

- Hardee, William J. *Rifle and Light Infantry Tactics.* 1855, and many wartime editions.
- Casey, Silas. *Infantry Tactics.* 1862.
- *The Ordnance Manual for the Use of the Officers of the United States Army.* 1861.

## Online Collections

- Springfield Armory National Historic Site (National Park Service).
- Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs Division, for period photographs of soldiers and their arms.
- Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Armed Forces History collection.
