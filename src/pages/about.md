*The Guns of the Civil War* is a reference to the small arms of the American Civil War, 1861–1865, written for **reenactors and living historians**. It aims to give the recruit and the veteran alike a clear account of each arm: how it was made, how it served, and how to carry it correctly today.

These pages are laid out in the manner of a newspaper of the 1860s, in tribute to the illustrated weeklies and daily broadsheets that carried the war to readers North and South.

## Corrections

This publication is a work in progress. If you find an error of fact, please open an issue or pull request on the project's GitHub repository, with a source for the correction.
